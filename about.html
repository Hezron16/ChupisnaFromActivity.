About and Help

Building Resilient Communities, Together

Our mission: to empower every Filipino with the knowledge and tools to be prepared for any disaster.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to use this website?

Start at Choose Hazard to learn what to do, then fill out My Checklist, review the Kit Guide and write your Family Plan.

What to do in an emergency?

Stay calm, move to a safe place and follow instructions from local officials. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies.

Who do I contact?

Dial 911 for emergencies. The Philippine Red Cross hotline is 143. Ask your barangay hall or local DRRMO about evacuation centers.

What is a resilient community?

A community whose members know their risks, plan together and help each other prepare for and recover from disasters.

Where can I get training?

The Philippine Red Cross, your local DRRMO and the Bureau of Fire Protection offer first aid, CPR and drills.

What sources does this use?

General guidance based on PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, NDRRMC, the Office of Civil Defense, the Philippine Red Cross and the Bureau of Fire Protection. Always follow their latest advisories.

What is HandaPH?

HandaPH is a website that helps Filipino families get ready for disasters. It gives simple, clear guidance so you know what to do before, during and after an emergency.

Who is it for?

Everyone: families, students, workers and communities who want to protect themselves and their loved ones.

What is on the website?

Guides for each hazard, a checklist you can tick off, a guide to packing an emergency kit, a family emergency plan, and answers to common questions.

Our mission

To empower every Filipino with the knowledge and tools to be prepared for any disaster.

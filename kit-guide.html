Emergency Kit Guide

Essential Go-Bag supplies for every disaster.

Water & Food

1 gallon (about 4 liters) per person per day, 3-day supply of non-perishable food.

First Aid

Bandages, antiseptic, medicines, gloves, mask.

Radio & Power

Battery or crank radio, flashlight, spare batteries, power bank.

Important Documents

ID, insurance, deeds, medical records in a waterproof bag.

Hygiene & Sanitation

Soap, sanitizer, wipes, garbage bags.

Clothing & Shelter

Sturdy shoes, warm clothes, emergency blanket, poncho.

Choose Your Hazard

Typhoon

Know the risks. Be ready.

  • Follow PAGASA bulletins and local announcements.
  • Secure windows and doors; charge phones and power banks.
  • Stay indoors and away from windows during the storm.
  • Evacuate when local officials tell you to.

Flood

Prepare before it rises.

  • Know your flood-prone areas and the nearest higher ground.
  • Move valuables and documents to a higher place.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Switch off the main power if water enters your home.

Earthquake

Know what to do.

  • Secure heavy furniture to the wall.
  • DROP, COVER and HOLD ON under a sturdy table.
  • Stay away from windows; do not use elevators.
  • Expect aftershocks and follow PHIVOLCS advisories.

Fire

Prevent and protect. Be prepared.

  • Install smoke alarms; never leave cooking unattended.
  • Plan two ways out of every room and practice.
  • Get low under smoke; feel doors before opening them.
  • Once out, stay out. Call 911 or the Bureau of Fire Protection.

In an emergency, call 911.
